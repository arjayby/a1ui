---
title: Components
description: Reusable UI components for building your own interfaces.
---

Animated components built with Motion, with live previews and editable source.

- [Menu](/components/menu): A shared highlight that travels between focused and selected items.
- [Dropdown Menu](/components/dropdown-menu): A shadcn dropdown with open and close transitions and a traveling highlight.
- [Confirm Button](/components/confirm-button): Hold to confirm with a directional color fill and configurable duration.
- [Copy Button](/components/copy-button): A copy icon and label that animate into a checkmark and success message.
- [Loading Button](/components/loading-button): Animated loading and success feedback for async actions and network requests.
- [Icon Toggle](/components/icon-toggle): Two icons that morph into each other on every click.
- [Switch](/components/switch): A thumb that squashes on press, springs across, and can rotate icons in and out.
- [Checkbox](/components/checkbox): A check that draws itself in, morphs from a dash, and can strike through its label.
- [Tabs](/components/tabs): A spring indicator that slides between tabs, with a hover highlight and direction-aware panels.
- [Slider](/components/slider): Magnetic stops that catch the thumb and pulse as you slide through them.
- [OTP Input](/components/otp-input): A one-time code field with 4, 6, or 8 slots and a traveling focus highlight.
- [Validated Input](/components/validated-input): A labeled field that checks its value with Zod and animates errors in.
- [Tag Input](/components/tag-input): A field for multiple tags with a two-step Backspace removal and an optional limit.
- [Password Input](/components/password-input): A password field with a strength meter that opens its requirements on hover, and a show/hide toggle.
- [Radio Group](/components/radio-group): One dot that slides between options, with a card variant whose highlighted border glides between cards.
- [Toggle Group](/components/toggle-group): A segmented control whose surface slides between items, or chips that pop in with a check.
- [Accordion](/components/accordion): Panels that glide open to their natural height, with staggered content and a chevron or plus-to-minus indicator.
- [Progress](/components/progress): A bar, ring, or step row that springs to each value while its percentage rolls like a counter.
- [Dialog](/components/dialog): A modal dialog that grows out of the button that opened it and shrinks back into it.
- [Alert Dialog](/components/alert-dialog): A confirmation dialog that springs in and shakes when you click outside it.
- [Drawer](/components/drawer): A bottom or side sheet that springs in, follows your drag, rubber-bands, and can scale the page behind it.
- [Toast](/components/toast): Toasts that stack as a deck, fan out on hover, swipe away, and morph from loading to success.
- [Tooltip](/components/tooltip): A tooltip that springs from its trigger and glides between neighbors in a toolbar.
- [Hover Card](/components/hover-card): A preview card that springs out of a link and staggers its contents in, on hover or keyboard focus.
- [Popover](/components/popover): A panel that springs out of its trigger, or a button that morphs into the panel.
- [Select](/components/select): A select whose list springs open from the trigger, with a traveling highlight, a drawn check, and a cross-fading value.
- [Command](/components/command): A command palette with a gliding highlight, reflowing results, and a ⌘K dialog.
- [Badge](/components/badge): A count that rolls like an odometer, a smooth resize, a pop in from zero, and a pulsing live dot.
